# Research and sources

Use research only when it improves understanding of the article's context, terminology, history, institutions, data, or consequences. Prefer, in order:

- official government, court, central-bank, regulator, legislature, treaty, or international-organization material;
- original research papers, filings, datasets, speeches, or institutional reports;
- established reference works and high-quality reporting for context or competing interpretations.

Record direct URLs in the note's Sources section. Label each source by role: article source, factual background, definition, data, or alternative perspective. Do not present a background source as proof of the NYT article's claims. For contested issues, describe the disagreement and attribute it. For time-sensitive facts, include the relevant date.
