# Note design

Use this order unless the article's shape clearly requires a small adjustment:

1. Article Metadata
2. Beginner Overview
3. Big Picture / Background
4. Key People, Organizations, Countries, and Concepts
5. Paragraph-by-paragraph and sentence-by-sentence Article Walkthrough
6. English Learning: vocabulary, chunks, grammar, sentence structures
7. Knowledge Expansion
8. Cause → Effect / Logic Chain
9. Why This Matters
10. What I Learned
11. Review Vocabulary & Chunks
12. Knowledge Connections
13. Sources

For each important sentence, retain the original English, give a natural Chinese translation, explain the most useful language points, and state the sentence's role in the paragraph. For straightforward sentences, combine these into a short entry. Do not omit a paragraph merely because it contains little vocabulary value: briefly account for its meaning and function.

Use `[[Knowledge/Concept Name]]` for durable concepts and `[[Articles/Readable Article Name]]` for related article notes. Link to an existing note before creating a new one.
